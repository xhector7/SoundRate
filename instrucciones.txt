🎧 SoundRate — App description

SoundRate is a modern music discovery and rating platform where users can explore tracks, share opinions, and connect through music.

The app allows users to browse a feed of songs, listen to previews, rate tracks, leave comments, and build a personal music profile based on their activity and taste. Each track becomes a social object, where community feedback helps surface trending and high-quality music.

Artists and listeners coexist in the same space: users can upload or interact with tracks, follow other users, and build a network around shared musical interests.

The goal of SoundRate is to turn music discovery into a social and interactive experience, where taste is shaped collectively and every track has a community behind it.

Key features:

Discover trending and personalized music
Rate and review tracks
Comment and interact with songs
Follow users and build a music network
Personal profile based on taste and activity
Clean, modern streaming-style interface

SoundRate is designed as a fast, modern SPA focused on UX, discovery, and community-driven music evaluation.
